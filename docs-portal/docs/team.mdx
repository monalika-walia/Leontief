## Organization & compliance posture

- **Entity:** Lemma Labs (Estonian OÜ registration in progress); the entity, not individuals,
  is the counterparty for any award or agreement.
- **KYC per contributor:** every contributor with commit or treasury access completes KYC, and is
  re-verified on any leadership or representative change. Team and entity are OFAC-clean.
- **Funds governance:** award funds held in a 2-of-3 team multisig; spend reported against
  deliverables per tranche (source of truth: `leontief-build-application.md`).
- **Commit policy:** commits are authored by the human team member of record; conventional
  commits; PRs note spec sections touched.
